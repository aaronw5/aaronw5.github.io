NeurIPS 2026First author

Patch Hierarchical Attention Transformer for Efficient Particle Jet Tagging

Aaron Wang, Zihan Zhao, Alan Xia, Chang Sun, Abhijith Gandrakota, Jennifer Ngadiuba, Richard Cavanaugh, Javier Duarte

A transformer for the LHC trigger's compute budget. A geometric message-passing module encodes local detector structure; hierarchical patch attention computes exact attention within small particle groups and passes information between groups through lightweight patch tokens. State-of-the-art among resource-constrained taggers on four benchmarks.

RAAAI Workshop @ NeurIPS 2026First author

On the Clock: Towards Punctual and Productive Time-Budgeted AI Agents

Aaron Wang, Neelabh Madan, Vlad Sobal, Matthew Trager, Michael Kleinman, Elman Mansimov, Wei Xia, Stefano Soatto

Can LLM agents respect a wall-clock time budget and use the time well? Told only the budget in the prompt, agents on MLE-Bench overran a 15-minute limit by 61% and finished on time in 15% of runs, because they cannot see elapsed time or predict how long actions take. Exposing time through the agent harness (a per-action clock, reminders, and an enforcement hook) brought them to budget with no loss in quality. GRPO training with a budget-aware reward then achieves near-exact adherence on Zork I, including on budgets not seen in training, but the trained policy learns when to stop rather than how to use extra time, filling it with repeated actions.

Sim2Sci Workshop @ NeurIPS 2026First author

Surrogate-Assisted Compact-Model Extraction for SKY130 CMOS at 77 K

Aaron Wang, et al.

A neural surrogate of the NGSpice circuit simulator, trained on simulated current-voltage curves, guides a bounded search for BSIM4 compact-model parameters that fit measured 77 K data on 18 SKY130 transistor geometries. Candidates are re-simulated in NGSpice before selection. Searches over 7 and 43 parameters lower fitting error against the released cards by 17% and 26%.

Why Is Attention Sparse in Particle Transformer?

Aaron Wang, Timothy Legge, Jacob Ortiz, Victor Limouzi, Zihan Zhao, Abhijith Gandrakota, Elham E. Khoda, Jennifer Ngadiuba, Javier Duarte, Richard Cavanaugh

ParT's attention is nearly binary. We separate two candidate causes, the attention mechanism and the physics-inspired interaction matrix, and find the attention mechanism is the main driver. ParT also picks out leptons from semileptonic top decays without any particle-ID input.

Interpreting Transformers for Jet Tagging

Aaron Wang, Abhijith Gandrakota, Jennifer Ngadiuba, Vivekanand Sahu, Priyansh Bhatnagar, Elham E. Khoda, Javier Duarte

Attention maps and particle-pair correlations on the η–φ plane show that each particle attends to at most one other, and that which particles and subjets the model focuses on depends on the decay. The model appears to learn traditional jet substructure observables on its own.

AutoML 2026 · Oral

SNAC-Pack 2.0: Scaled-Out Surrogate Neural Architecture Codesign

Jason Weitz, Dmitri Demler, Benjamin Hawks, Aaron Wang, Nhan Tran, Javier Duarte

An open-source framework for hardware-aware neural architecture search with FPGA deployment. A surrogate model estimates resource use and latency for each candidate so the search does not wait on synthesis, followed by quantization-aware training, pruning and hls4ml synthesis. On LHC jet classification and superconducting qubit readout it finds compact models on the accuracy-resource Pareto front.

Projects

Distilling jet taggers into physics equations

The latent space of JEDI-linear (an FPGA-deployable real-time tagger) and ParT (the Particle Transformer) rewritten as if-statements on jet physics quantities. For JEDI-linear, the formulas reproduce the network's decision on 94% of jets and explain 92% of each neuron's variance, versus 16% for an untrained copy.

What is the tiny trigger tagger doing?

Every neuron of JEDI-linear, a quantized real-time jet tagger, as an exact formula of pT, Δη, Δφ, matched to a physics quantity. Sparse autoencoders, transcoders, and decision trees distilled from the network, explorable jet by jet.

ML for cryogenic transistor modeling

Neural surrogates of SkyWater 130 nm transistor I-V curves at 77 K extract BSIM4 parameters, validated in NGSpice. Lower error than the published parameters on all 18 devices. Basis of the Sim2Sci 2026 paper.
